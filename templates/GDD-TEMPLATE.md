# [Project] — design and delivery contract

Use this for a substantial design whose decisions need to survive iteration or a change of session. When the owner chooses a Design Freeze, mark the agreed baseline FINAL and record its date. Keep exploration in conversation until then. An existing specification can serve this purpose.

## Experience and scope

- Intended audience, core experience and distinguishing idea
- User journey or gameplay loop, inputs, feedback, outcomes and recovery
- Rules, content, screens, states, persistence and important edge cases
- Existing behavior to preserve, exclusions and delegated decisions

## Presentation and constraints

- Approved visual references and exact source asset paths, licenses and editable inputs
- Observable finish criteria for composition, motion, interaction and sound
- Platforms, input, language, accessibility, storage, network and privacy requirements
- Budget, dependency constraints, likely feasibility issues and approved fallbacks

## Acceptance and delivery

- Project repository, adopted standards pins, Release or Experiment scope
- Representative success and failure scenarios, release-critical checks and allowable limitations
- Actions actually authorized, their exact targets and visibility

| Action | Target and permission |
| --- | --- |
| Source changes and branch pushes | [repo and branch rules] |
| Merge | [branch and required checks] |
| Project deployment | [host, route and exposure] |
| Separate catalog edit and publication | [named portal, section, artwork and route] |
| Services, data sharing and spending | [scope and ceiling, $0 by default] |

Omit irrelevant fields. An empty permission field grants no authority. Record later changes beside the decisions they supersede.
